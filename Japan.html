Tokyo, Japan

A futuristic metropolis that seamlessly blends cutting-edge technology and pop culture with ancient Japanese traditions and serene temples.

Best Time to Visit

March to May (Cherry Blossom Season) and September to November

Average Temperature

10°C - 20°C

Starting Price

$1,500

Famous Places

Tokyo Tower

An iconic communications and observation tower inspired by the Eiffel Tower.

Senso-ji Temple

Tokyo's oldest and most significant Buddhist temple.

Shibuya Crossing

The world's busiest pedestrian intersection and a symbol of urban Tokyo.

Meiji Shrine

A peaceful Shinto shrine surrounded by a lush forest in the middle of the city.

Activities

  • City Tours: Sightseeing tours covering futuristic districts and historic sites.
  • Shopping: Shopping for electronics, anime merchandise, and fashion in Akihabara and Shibuya.
  • Museums: Exploring interactive digital art museums (like teamLab) and tech centers.
  • Restaurants: Enjoying world-class sushi, authentic ramen, and Japanese street food.
  • Adventure Activities: Theme park visits like Tokyo Disneyland and VR gaming experiences.
