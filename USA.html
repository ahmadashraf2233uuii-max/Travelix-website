New York, USA

"The City That Never Sleeps," serving as a global capital for finance, fashion, culture, art, and entertainment.

Best Time to Visit

April to June, and September to November

Average Temperature

10°C - 22°C

Starting Price

$1,300

Famous Places

Statue of Liberty

The world-famous monument symbolizing freedom on Liberty Island.

Times Square

The vibrant heart of Manhattan, famous for bright LED billboards and energy.

Central Park

A massive green urban oasis providing scenic paths and lakes amidst skyscrapers.

Empire State Building

An iconic Art Deco skyscraper with open-air observation decks.

Activities

  • City Tours: Neighborhood walking tours through SoHo, Brooklyn, and Greenwich Village.
  • Shopping: Flagship retail shopping along Fifth Avenue and SoHo boutiques.
  • Museums: Visiting MoMA, The Met, and the American Museum of Natural History.
  • Adventure Activities: Broadway theatre shows, cycling in Central Park, and helicopter city tours.
  • Restaurants: Sampling famous New York-style pizza, bagels, and Michelin-star dining.
