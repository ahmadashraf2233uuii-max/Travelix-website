Zurich, Switzerland

Switzerland's vibrant global financial center, located at the edge of Lake Zurich with backdrop views of the Swiss Alps, known for luxury and cleanliness.

Best Time to Visit

June to August (Summer) or December to February (Winter skiing)

Average Temperature

8°C - 20°C

Starting Price

$1,200

Famous Places

Lake Zurich

A pristine lake offering scenic cruises and promenade walks.

Bahnhofstrasse

One of the world's most exclusive shopping avenues for luxury watches and jewelry.

Old Town (Altstadt)

Historic district filled with medieval guild houses, narrow lanes, and cafes.

Grossmünster Church

A twin-towered Romanesque church that stands as a primary landmark.

Activities

  • City Tours: Walking tours around the historic Old Town and Limmat riverbank.
  • Shopping: Shopping for Swiss watches, high-end fashion, and Swiss chocolates.
  • Museums: Visiting the Swiss National Museum and Kunsthaus Zurich.
  • Adventure Activities: Boat cruises on Lake Zurich, Alpine hiking, and day trips to nearby ski slopes.
