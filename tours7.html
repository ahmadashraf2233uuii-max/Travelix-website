Santorini, Greece Tour

Discover the breathtaking white-washed houses, blue domes, and stunning sunsets with our exclusive tour package to Santorini, Greece!

Included Services

  • Comfortable 4-star cliffside hotel accommodation with a caldera view
  • Daily traditional Greek breakfast included
  • Private airport transfers and island transportation

Main Activities

  • Guided walking tour of the famous cliffside towns of Oia and Fira
  • A romantic sunset catamaran cruise across the Aegean Sea
  • Exclusive wine-tasting experience featuring local volcanic wines

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Fira

Arrive, check in to the hotel, and enjoy an evening stroll through Fira's caldera-view streets.

Day 2: Oia Village

Guided walking tour of the iconic village of Oia, famous for its sunsets.

Day 3: Catamaran Cruise

Romantic sunset catamaran cruise across the Aegean Sea.

Day 4: Wine & Culture

Exclusive wine-tasting experience featuring local volcanic wines.

Day 5: Departure

Free morning at leisure, then transfer to the airport.
