Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland Tour

Discover the magical alpine valley of waterfalls and majestic mountains with our exclusive tour package to Lauterbrunnen, Switzerland!

Included Services

  • Cozy 4-star alpine chalet accommodation with mountain views
  • Daily traditional Swiss breakfast included
  • Private guided transfers and Swiss Travel Pass for seamless transportation

Main Activities

  • Guided hiking tour through the alpine valley and famous waterfall trails
  • Scenic mountain railway and cable car experience with panoramic Alps views
  • Exclusive visit to the breathtaking Trümmelbach Falls

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Valley Views

Arrive at the chalet, and enjoy an evening walk through the valley admiring the waterfalls.

Day 2: Alpine Hiking

Guided hiking tour along the valley's famous waterfall trails.

Day 3: Mountain Railway

Scenic mountain railway and cable car journey with panoramic views of the Swiss Alps.

Day 4: Trümmelbach Falls

Exclusive visit to the Trümmelbach Falls, followed by a free afternoon in the village.

Day 5: Departure

Free morning at leisure, then transfer via the Swiss Travel Pass to the airport.
