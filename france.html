Paris, France

Paris is the capital of France and one of the world's most famous cities for culture, art, fashion, and gastronomy. It is renowned for its iconic historical landmarks and romantic streets along the Seine River.

Best Time to Visit

April to June (Spring) or September to October (Autumn)

Average Temperature

12°C - 22°C (up to 25°C in summer)

Starting Price

$1,200

Famous Places

Eiffel Tower

The most famous symbol of Paris and a masterpiece of modern engineering.

Louvre Museum

The world's largest art museum, home to the Mona Lisa.

Notre-Dame Cathedral

A classic Gothic architectural masterpiece located in the heart of Paris.

Champs-Élysées

A world-famous avenue renowned for luxury shopping, cafes, and ending at the Arc de Triomphe.

Activities

  • City Tours: Guided tours to explore historical neighborhoods and city highlights.
  • Shopping: Luxury shopping at famous boutiques and department stores.
  • Museums: Visiting world-class art galleries and historical museums.
  • Restaurants: Experiencing authentic French cuisine and sidewalk cafes.
  • Adventure Activities: Boat cruises along the Seine River and outdoor city adventures.
