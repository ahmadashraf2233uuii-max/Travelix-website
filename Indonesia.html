Bali, Indonesia

A tropical paradise island famous for its sandy beaches, lush terraced rice fields, ancient cliffside temples, and relaxing wellness culture.

Best Time to Visit

April to October (Dry season)

Average Temperature

24°C - 30°C

Starting Price

$1,000

Famous Places

Tanah Lot Temple

An ancient Hindu shrine perched atop an offshore rock formation.

Tegallalang Rice Terraces

Emerald-green terraced fields offering breathtaking tropical valley views.

Uluwatu Temple

A sea temple sitting on a high cliff overlooking the ocean, famous for Kecak dance shows.

Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary

A natural forest sanctuary inhabited by hundreds of long-tailed macaques.

Activities

  • City Tours: Exploring island waterfalls, traditional villages, and volcanic landscapes.
  • Shopping: Shopping for handmade woodcarvings and local arts in Ubud Market.
  • Museums: Visiting Neka Art Museum and Blanco Renaissance Museum.
  • Restaurants: Beachfront seafood dining at Jimbaran Bay and local Balinese warungs.
  • Adventure Activities: Surfing, scuba diving, island swings, and volcano hiking.
