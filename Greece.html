Santorini, Greece

One of the most breathtaking Aegean islands, celebrated for its whitewashed cliffside houses, blue-domed churches, and world-famous sunsets.

Best Time to Visit

Late April to October

Average Temperature

18°C - 27°C

Starting Price

$980

Famous Places

Oia Village

Iconic village famous for its blue-domed architecture and sunset vistas.

Fira (Capital)

The cliffside capital filled with shops, cafes, and sweeping caldera views.

Red Beach

A unique volcanic beach surrounded by soaring red cliff formations.

Akrotiri Archaeological Site

An ancient Minoan Bronze Age settlement preserved beneath volcanic ash.

Activities

  • City Tours: Exploring picturesque villages perched along the volcanic caldera.
  • Shopping: Browsing local artisan jewelry, ceramics, and boutique fashion.
  • Museums: Visiting the Museum of Prehistoric Thera and Maritime Museum.
  • Adventure Activities: Catamaran cruises around the volcanic caldera, scuba diving, and volcanic hot spring swimming.
  • Restaurants: Enjoying fresh Mediterranean seafood and Greek salads overlooking the sea.
