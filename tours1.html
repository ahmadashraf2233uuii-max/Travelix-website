Paris, France Tour

Explore the City of Light with our exclusive and unforgettable tour package to Paris, France!

Included Services

  • Comfortable 4-star hotel accommodation located in the heart of Paris
  • Daily authentic French breakfast included
  • Professional English-speaking tour guide throughout the journey

Main Activities

  • Guided tour of the iconic Eiffel Tower and priority access
  • A romantic cruise along the Seine River at sunset
  • Exclusive entry tickets to the world-famous Louvre Museum

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Eiffel Tower

Arrive in Paris, check in to the hotel, and enjoy an evening guided visit to the Eiffel Tower with priority access.

Day 2: Louvre & Art

Full day exploring the Louvre Museum with a guide, followed by a walk through the Tuileries Garden.

Day 3: Seine River & Old Paris

Morning walking tour of Notre-Dame and the Latin Quarter, followed by a sunset cruise along the Seine River.

Day 4: Montmartre & Local Life

Explore the artists' district of Montmartre, visit Sacré-Cœur, and enjoy a free afternoon for shopping.

Day 5: Departure

Final French breakfast, free morning at leisure, then transfer to the airport.
