London, UK

The capital of the United Kingdom, renowned for its royal heritage, rich history, world-class theatre scene, and diverse global culture.

Best Time to Visit

May to September

Average Temperature

11°C - 21°C

Starting Price

$1,500

Famous Places

Big Ben & Palace of Westminster

The iconic clock tower and parliament buildings along the River Thames.

London Eye

A giant observation wheel offering panoramic views across London's skyline.

The British Museum

A world-renowned museum dedicated to human history, art, and culture.

Tower Bridge

A historic bascule and suspension bridge spanning the River Thames.

Activities

  • City Tours: Sightseeing on iconic red double-decker buses or river cruises.
  • Shopping: High-street shopping on Oxford Street and luxury browsing at Harrods.
  • Museums: Visiting the Natural History Museum, Science Museum, and Tate Modern.
  • Restaurants: Sampling traditional Fish & Chips, Sunday Roast, and international cuisine.
  • Adventure Activities: Watching West End musical shows and speedboat tours on the Thames.
