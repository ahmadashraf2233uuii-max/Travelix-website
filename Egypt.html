Cairo, Egypt

The vibrant capital of ancient Pharaonic civilization, known as the "City of a Thousand Minarets," offering a captivating mix of history along the Nile River.

Best Time to Visit

October to April (Winter and early Spring)

Average Temperature

15°C - 26°C

Starting Price

$600

Famous Places

Pyramids of Giza & The Sphinx

The last remaining Wonder of the Ancient World and Egypt's defining landmark.

The Grand Egyptian Museum (GEM)

Home to the world's most extensive collection of ancient Pharaonic treasures, including Tutankhamun's relics.

Khan El-Khalili Bazaar

A historic medieval souk famous for handicrafts, spices, and traditional cafes like El-Fishawy.

Citadel of Saladin

An Islamic fortification housing the Mosque of Muhammad Ali with panoramic city views.

Activities

  • City Tours: Exploring Islamic and Coptic Cairo's historical monuments.
  • Shopping: Buying handmade souvenirs, copperware, and jewelry at traditional bazaars.
  • Museums: Visiting the National Museum of Egyptian Civilization and Coptic Museum.
  • Restaurants: Trying authentic Egyptian dishes like Koshary and grilled delights along the Nile.
  • Adventure Activities: Camel and horseback riding around the Pyramids, and Felucca rides on the Nile.
