Dubai, UAE

A global hub of luxury and modern innovation, featuring record-breaking skyscrapers, world-class shopping, and desert adventures.

Best Time to Visit

November to March (Mild winter season)

Average Temperature

20°C - 28°C

Starting Price

$1,300

Famous Places

Burj Khalifa

The world's tallest building offering breathtaking panoramic observation decks.

The Dubai Mall

A massive shopping and entertainment complex housing the Dubai Aquarium & Fountain.

Palm Jumeirah

A world-famous man-made palm-shaped island with luxury resorts.

Museum of the Future

An architectural marvel showcasing innovative futuristic technologies.

Activities

  • City Tours: Luxury city tours and sightseeing via open-top buses or supercar rentals.
  • Shopping: World-class shopping in mega malls and traditional Gold Souks.
  • Museums: Exploring the Museum of the Future and Al Shindagha Museum.
  • Restaurants: Dining at Michelin-starred restaurants and underwater eateries.
  • Adventure Activities: Desert safaris, dune bashing, skydiving over Palm Jumeirah, and waterpark sports.
