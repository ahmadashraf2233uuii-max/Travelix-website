Pyramids of Giza, Egypt Tour

Discover the ancient wonders of the world with our exclusive and unforgettable tour package to the Pyramids of Giza, Egypt!

Included Services

  • Comfortable 4-star hotel accommodation with a direct view of the pyramids
  • Daily traditional Egyptian breakfast included
  • Private air-conditioned transportation throughout the trip

Main Activities

  • Guided tour of the Great Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx with an expert Egyptologist
  • An exciting camel ride around the Giza plateau with panoramic desert views
  • Exclusive entry ticket to explore inside the pyramids

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Giza Plateau

Arrive in Cairo, check in with a direct pyramid view, and enjoy a sunset visit to the Giza Plateau.

Day 2: Pyramids & Sphinx

Guided tour inside the Great Pyramid with an expert Egyptologist and a close-up visit to the Sphinx.

Day 3: Desert Adventure

Camel ride around the Giza plateau with panoramic desert views, followed by a traditional lunch.

Day 4: Cairo Highlights

Visit the Grand Egyptian Museum and explore the Khan El-Khalili Bazaar.

Day 5: Departure

Free morning at leisure, then transfer to the airport.
