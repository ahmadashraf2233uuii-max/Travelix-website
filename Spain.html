Barcelona, Spain

The capital of Catalonia, world-famous for Antoni Gaudí's whimsical architecture, Mediterranean beaches, and lively artistic and sports culture.

Best Time to Visit

May to June, and September to October

Average Temperature

16°C - 24°C

Starting Price

$1,000

Famous Places

La Sagrada Família

Gaudí's unfinished masterpiece basilica and the city's main icon.

Park Güell

A colorful public park system composed of vibrant mosaic architecture.

La Rambla Street

A bustling pedestrian street lined with cafes, performers, and local markets.

Casa Batlló

A surrealist residential building designed by Gaudí featuring organic shapes.

Activities

  • City Tours: Architectural walking tours focusing on Modernisme design.
  • Shopping: High-end shopping on Passeig de Gràcia and fresh food at La Boqueria Market.
  • Museums: Visiting the Picasso Museum and the National Art Museum of Catalonia.
  • Restaurants: Tasting tapas, fresh seafood, and traditional Spanish Paella.
  • Adventure Activities: Water sports and sunbathing at Barceloneta Beach.
