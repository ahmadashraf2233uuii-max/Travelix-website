Istanbul, Turkey

The transcontinental city straddling both Europe and Asia, rich with Byzantine and Ottoman history, vibrant bazaars, and scenic waterways.

Best Time to Visit

April to May, and September to November

Average Temperature

13°C - 22°C

Starting Price

$800

Famous Places

Hagia Sophia

A world-famous architectural wonder bridging Byzantine and Ottoman heritage.

Sultanahmet Mosque (Blue Mosque)

Iconic mosque renowned for its six minarets and intricate blue interior tiles.

The Grand Bazaar

One of the oldest and largest covered markets selling carpets, spices, and crafts.

Topkapi Palace

The primary residence of Ottoman Sultans for centuries, featuring holy relics.

Activities

  • City Tours: Exploring Sultanahmet, Galata, and Taksim historic quarters.
  • Shopping: Bargaining at the Grand Bazaar, Spice Market, and İstiklal Avenue.
  • Museums: Visiting the Istanbul Archaeology Museums and Dolmabahçe Palace.
  • Restaurants: Tasting Turkish Kebabs, Mezes, Baklava, and authentic Turkish Tea.
  • Adventure Activities: Scenic Bosphorus strait cruises and hamam spa experiences.
