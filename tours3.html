The Colosseum, Rome Tour

Step back in time to the glory of the Roman Empire with our exclusive and unforgettable tour package to the historic Colosseum in Rome, Italy!

Included Services

  • Comfortable 4-star hotel accommodation located near the historic center
  • Daily authentic Italian breakfast included
  • Private airport transfers and guided city transportation

Main Activities

  • Skip-the-line guided walking tour of the Roman Colosseum with an expert historian
  • Explore the ancient Roman Forum and Palatine Hill
  • A traditional Italian food and gelato tasting experience in downtown Rome

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Rome by Night

Arrive, check in to the hotel, and enjoy an evening walking tour through the illuminated historic streets.

Day 2: Colosseum & Forum

Skip-the-line tour of the Colosseum with an expert historian, followed by the Roman Forum and Palatine Hill.

Day 3: Vatican & Art

Guided visit to Vatican City, St. Peter's Basilica, and the Sistine Chapel.

Day 4: Taste of Rome

Italian food and gelato tasting tour through the historic center.

Day 5: Departure

Free morning for last-minute shopping, then transfer to the airport.
