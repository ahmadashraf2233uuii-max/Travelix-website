Himeji Castle, Japan Tour

Experience the breathtaking beauty of cherry blossoms and ancient Japanese architecture with our exclusive tour package to Himeji Castle, Japan!

Included Services

  • Comfortable traditional-style hotel accommodation with modern amenities
  • Daily traditional Japanese breakfast included
  • Private guided tours and seamless bullet train transportation pass

Main Activities

  • Guided tour of the historic UNESCO World Heritage Himeji Castle and its gardens
  • Traditional boat ride along the castle moat under blooming cherry blossoms
  • Authentic Japanese tea ceremony experience with local masters

Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival & Himeji

Arrive, take the bullet train to Himeji, check in to the hotel, and relax for the evening.

Day 2: Himeji Castle

Full guided tour of the UNESCO World Heritage Himeji Castle and its surrounding gardens.

Day 3: Cherry Blossoms & Moat

Traditional boat ride along the castle moat beneath the blooming cherry blossoms.

Day 4: Tea Ceremony & Culture

Authentic Japanese tea ceremony experience with local masters, followed by free time to explore.

Day 5: Departure

Bullet train transfer, free morning, then transfer to the airport.
