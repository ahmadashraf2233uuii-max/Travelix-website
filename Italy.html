Rome, Italy

The capital of Italy and the heart of the ancient Roman Empire. It seamlessly blends ancient historic ruins with vibrant Italian street life and iconic cuisine.

Best Time to Visit

April to May, and September to October

Average Temperature

15°C - 25°C

Starting Price

$1,100

Famous Places

The Colosseum

A monumental Roman amphitheater that hosted historic gladiatorial contests.

Trevi Fountain

The most iconic Baroque fountain in the world, famous for coin-tossing traditions.

The Pantheon

An exceptionally preserved ancient Roman temple with a world-renowned dome.

Vatican City & Museums

The heart of the Roman Catholic Church, home to St. Peter's Basilica and the Sistine Chapel.

Activities

  • City Tours: Walking tours through ancient ruins and cobblestone streets.
  • Shopping: Shopping for Italian fashion around the Spanish Steps.
  • Museums: Exploring ancient Roman artifacts and Renaissance art.
  • Restaurants: Tasting authentic Italian pizza, pasta, and gelato.
  • Adventure Activities: Vespa scooter tours around the city and cycling excursions.
